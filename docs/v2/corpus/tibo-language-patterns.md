## Supported conclusions

1. Explicit action + completed tense + scope remains the strongest completion evidence.
2. Future phrases are actionable but do not mean completion and can be revised.
3. Incident compensation can still be a Full Reset; incident reason is not a Special mechanism.
4. Banked/reset-card effects are Special even when mentioned beside a Full Reset.
5. Milestones, gifts, button jokes, sleep/rejuvenation, and celebration language need action/scope context.
6. Product launches and quota increases are not Resets merely because a source site labels them.
7. Same-post completion must not be counted as advance-warning performance.

## Quantitative boundary

The set contains 20 reviewed cases: 15 Full-linked, two Special-linked, and three `UNKNOWN`/context cases
when counted by stored outcome type. It is not a complete sample of Tibo posts, so it does not support a
phrase probability, false-positive rate, 72-hour negative rate, or coverage percentage. Historical replay
continues to exclude outcomes after the replay cutoff.
